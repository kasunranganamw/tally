Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Tally is built to work without collecting data about you or your family beyond what the app needs to function. This page explains exactly what that means.

What Tally stores

The behavior, reward, and household information you enter is stored on your device, and — if you enable iCloud sync — in your own private iCloud account. We do not have access to it, cannot see it, and do not store a copy of it on any server we operate.

Purchases

Tally Plus subscriptions are processed by Apple and by RevenueCat, our subscription infrastructure provider. RevenueCat receives purchase and device identifiers needed to validate your subscription. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Crash reports

Tally uses Firebase Crashlytics to learn when the app crashes so we can fix it. If the app crashes, technical details (what the app was doing, device model, OS version) are sent to Firebase (Google). This is not linked to your identity and is never used for advertising or tracking. See Firebase's privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no ad tracking.
  • No selling or sharing your data with third parties for marketing.

Children's privacy

Tally is designed for use by parents and guardians. Any information about a child (name, logged behavior) is entered by the parent, stored as described above, and never collected by us directly from a child.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will change too. Continued use of Tally after an update means you accept the revised policy.
